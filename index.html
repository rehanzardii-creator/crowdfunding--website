About HopeFund

This website is a college project demonstrating a responsive crowdfunding platform for social causes. The design focuses on simplicity, accessibility and easy navigation for both mobile and laptop users.

Why Crowdfunding?

Crowdfunding allows many people to contribute small amounts toward a common goal. It can connect donors with meaningful social initiatives and encourage community participation.
